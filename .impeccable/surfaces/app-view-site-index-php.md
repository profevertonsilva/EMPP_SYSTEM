# Public site (/)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a researcher who makes electrospun membranes, arriving cold. Action: create a free account (secondary: sign in). Proof on hand: the working tool and its four-step path; filtration numbers computed with EMPP's own equations. Constraints: English; no invented affiliations, DOIs, counts or testimonials; sample values labelled illustrative; research credits render only when supplied in `App/View/site/research.php`. `/` replaces the login as entry; signed-in visitors get "Open dashboard".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the record a study produces, filled in for one sample membrane; it refuses the icon-card feature grid and the stock hero screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: The incumbent EMPP instrument world: logo blue #34498c on a deep #1a2547 field with the fibre texture, stage black #0e1320 behind the micrograph, white sheet with hairline rules, IBM Plex Sans, Plex Mono for every measured value.

STORY: The visitor sees what a study returns, reads which step produced each value, believes the path is real because the filtration figures are computed live, and signs up to run their own.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Deep blue field, top 60%: logo and nav, headline left at display scale, two actions below it. The study sheet rises from the field across the fold: micrograph left, parameters, features and porosity readout centre, numbered margin notes right.

FORM: Study record, position 2 of 7, seed key 7dbeaa7a. Signature: the sheet fills in once, step by step, as a study run.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
